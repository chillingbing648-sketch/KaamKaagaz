🗺️ADMISSION ROADMAPS

Visual Journey: Step-by-Step

Understand exactly what happens at every stage — from registration and verification to allotment and final college confirmation.

11th / FYJCAY 2026–27

Maharashtra 11th / FYJC Admission Roadmap

Standard step-by-step journey from 10th result to junior college confirmation

✓ Source-Backed Sequence
1

Portal Registration & Login Creation

Step 1 of 6

Visit mahafyjcadmissions.in, select your region (e.g. Mumbai / Pune), and create your student account using your 10th seat number or manual entry.

🗂️ What You Need Ready:

10th Roll Number, Mother's name, active mobile number for OTP.

mahafyjcadmissions.inOpen FYJC Portal↗
2

Fill Part 1 Form (Personal & Reservation Details)

Step 2 of 6

Enter personal details, address, category (General, SC, ST, OBC, EWS, etc.), and choose verification mode (Secondary School E-Scrutiny or Guidance Center).

🗂️ What You Need Ready:

10th Marksheet, Domicile/Birth Certificate, Caste Certificate & Validity (if reserved category).

3

Document Verification & Approval of Part 1

Step 3 of 6

Your secondary school or designated guidance center verifies your uploaded documents online. Once approved, your Part 1 status becomes 'Verified'.

🗂️ What You Need Ready:

Keep original certificates ready if flagged for clarification.

⚡ What Happens After You Submit:

Check your portal dashboard daily until you see the green 'VERIFIED' badge. Do NOT wait until Part 2 opens to fix verification errors.

4

Fill Part 2 Option Form (Junior College Choices)

Step 4 of 6

Select stream (Arts / Science / Commerce), medium of instruction, and add minimum 1 to maximum 10 junior college preferences in order of choice.

🗂️ What You Need Ready:

Previous year's cut-off marks for selected colleges to set realistic preferences.

5

Merit List & Round-wise Seat Allotment

Step 5 of 6

The system runs algorithmic allocation based on your merit marks, category quota, and preference order. Portal displays 'Allotted Junior College'.

🗂️ What You Need Ready:

Student Login ID to view Provisional Allotment Letter.

⚡ What Happens After You Submit:

RULE: If you are allotted your 1st PREFERENCE college, you MUST take admission. If you reject 1st preference, you are blocked from the next regular round!

6

Report to Allotted College & Confirm Admission

Step 6 of 6

Visit the college with original documents and required admission fees before the deadline. The college clicks 'Admit' on their portal to generate your official receipt.

🗂️ What You Need Ready:

Original 10th Marksheet, Original School Leaving Certificate (LC), Caste Validity (if applicable), Allotment Letter printout.

Always save payment receipts and system-generated allotment forms as PDFs.

💡REAL-WORLD TROUBLESHOOTING

Common Admission Problems & Concrete Next Steps

Real-world solutions for stuck applications, pending caste validity, name discrepancies, payment timeouts, and seat upgrade decisions.

🔍 The Situation:

Students get overwhelmed by dozens of portals (Samarth, CET Cell, FYJC, CDOE, college sites) and don't know where to start.

📖 Plain Language Explanation:

Admission portals are divided strictly by course authority: (1) 11th Std -> mahafyjcadmissions.in; (2) Professional degrees (Engg, MBA, Pharmacy, Law, BBA/BMS) -> cetcell.mahacet.org; (3) Traditional Mumbai University degrees (BA, B.Com, B.Sc) -> muadmission.samarth.edu.in PLUS each individual college site; (4) Distance / Online -> mucdoeadm.samarth.edu.in; (5) PhD -> uomphd.mu.ac.in.

🎯 What you should do next:
  1. Use our Admission Finder tool above to get the exact direct portal URL for your course.
  2. Never apply on unofficial consultancy or third-party coaching websites posing as admission portals.
  3. Bookmark the verified portal and follow official schedules strictly.
📖PLAIN-LANGUAGE VOCABULARY

Admission Terms Demystified

Plain language first. Official bureaucratic terminology second.

CAP

Official Term

Centralised Admission Process (CAP)

Plain English Meaning:

A single, unified state admission system where students apply to many colleges together through one online form instead of visiting each college separately.

How It Affects You:

You fill your college preference list once; the system matches your merit marks against college cut-offs automatically.

Real Example: Instead of standing in lines at 10 different engineering colleges, you fill 10 choices in CAP and get allotted the best one matching your rank.

CET

Official Term

Common Entrance Test (CET)

Plain English Meaning:

A competitive state-level entrance examination used to rank students for admission into professional undergraduate and postgraduate degrees.

How It Affects You:

Your CET percentile score determines your State Merit Rank, which is the primary factor deciding which college you can get.

Merit Number

Official Term

State General Merit Rank (SML) / Category Merit Rank

Plain English Meaning:

Your official position in the rank list of all applicants in the entire state (like roll-call order from 1 to the last student).

How It Affects You:

Higher merit rank (closer to #1) means you get first priority during seat allocation over students with lower ranks.

Seat Allotment

Official Term

Provisional / Final Seat Allotment

Plain English Meaning:

The decision by the admission portal assigning you a specific seat in a specific college and branch for that round.

How It Affects You:

Once allotted, you must download the Allotment Letter and choose whether to Freeze or take Betterment.

E-Scrutiny

Official Term

Online Document Scrutiny & Verification

Plain English Meaning:

Verification of your uploaded certificates online by government scrutiny officers without you needing to travel anywhere in person.

How It Affects You:

Saves time and travel. If any scan is unclear, the officer sends an online query that you must answer in 48 hours.

APAAR ID

Official Term

Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry

Plain English Meaning:

A 12-digit lifelong unique digital education roll number for Indian students under NEP 2020.

How It Affects You:

Required during university pre-admission registration and stays with you from school through Ph.D.

PRN

Official Term

Permanent Registration Number (PRN)

Plain English Meaning:

A unique permanent identification number issued by the university once your admission is officially finalized.

How It Affects You:

Used for all university exam hall tickets, marksheet generation, and degree convocation.

TFWS

Official Term

Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS)

Plain English Meaning:

A government merit scheme providing 100% waiver of tuition fees in professional degree/diploma courses for meritorious students with family income under ₹8 Lakhs.

How It Affects You:

Separate TFWS choice codes exist in the CAP option form. If allotted, you only pay basic development and exam fees.
