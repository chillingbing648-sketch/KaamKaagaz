Income Certificate (Maharashtra) · What you need

7/12 & 8-A Extract (Farmers)

Official land revenue records for agricultural income assessment.

Checklist Status

Open my checklist →

What is it?

Agricultural land records proving farming income.

Why do I need it?

Used by the Revenue Department to assess annual crop income.

ℹ️ Plain-language explanation from KAAMKAAGAZ, not an official legal statement.

✔️

What can you use?

This list is based on official rules. Requirements can vary depending on your situation.

  • Digitally signed 7/12 extract
  • 8-A extract
📋

Format & preparation

📄 Submission type

Upload scanned copy or digital PDF on portal.

✍️ Self-attestation

Signed by landowner.

💻 Digital / scan copy

Digitally signed Mahabhumi PDF accepted.

📐 File format

PDF or JPEG (under 256 KB).

⏳ Validity / recentness

Extract should be recent (current financial year).

ℹ️ Requirements can change. Always check the official website before final submission.

📜From official guidelines

Digital 7/12 extracts with QR code from mahabhumi.gov.in are accepted directly (Govt of Maharashtra GR).
