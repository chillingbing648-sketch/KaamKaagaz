Aadhaar Card · What you need

Proof of Address (PoA)

An official document proving your current residential address.

Checklist Status

Open my checklist →

What is it?

Establishes where you reside. The Aadhaar letter will be dispatched by India Post to this exact physical address.

Why do I need it?

To associate your biometric identity with your verified residential location.

ℹ️ Plain-language explanation from KAAMKAAGAZ, not an official legal statement.

✔️

What can you use?

This list is based on official rules. Requirements can vary depending on your situation.

  • Electricity Bill (not older than 3 months)
  • Water Bill (not older than 3 months)
  • Bank Statement / Passbook with photo & branch stamp (not older than 3 months)
  • Valid Registered Rent Agreement
  • Voter ID Card
  • Indian Passport
📋

Format & preparation

📄 Submission type

Original bill / passbook for scanning at Kendra, or uploaded on myAadhaar.

✍️ Self-attestation

Self-attested copy required if uploading online.

💻 Digital / scan copy

Original digital e-bill PDF downloaded from official electricity/water utility portal is accepted.

📐 File format

Color PDF or JPG under 2MB.

⏳ Validity / recentness

Utility bills and bank statements must be less than 90 days old.

💡 What if I don't have this?

Family members residing together can use Head of Family (HoF) based address update via Aadhaar OTP authorization.

ℹ️ Requirements can change. Always check the official website before final submission.

📜From official guidelines

Utility bills must not be older than 3 months from the date of application.
