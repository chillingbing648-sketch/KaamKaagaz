Passport · What you need

Proof of date of birth

Authoritative document stating your exact date and place of birth.

Checklist Status

Open my checklist →

What is it?

An official record of your birth date and place.

Why do I need it?

Printed inside the passport booklet to verify your legal age and identity.

ℹ️ Plain-language explanation from KAAMKAAGAZ, not an official legal statement.

✔️

What can you use?

This list is based on official rules. Requirements can vary depending on your situation.

  • Birth Certificate from Municipal Corporation / Gram Panchayat
  • 10th standard pass certificate / School Leaving Certificate
  • Aadhaar card
  • PAN card
  • Driving Licence
📋

Format & preparation

📄 Submission type

Original document mandatory at PSK appointment + 1 self-attested photocopy.

✍️ Self-attestation

Required on photocopy.

💻 Digital / scan copy

Accepted for portal upload (PDF under 1 MB).

📐 File format

Original hard copy at PSK + clear photocopy.

⏳ Validity / recentness

Date and place of birth must be clearly legible and match your application form exactly.

💡 What if I don't have this?

If you don't possess a municipal birth certificate, your 10th School Leaving Certificate (TC), Aadhaar card, or PAN card can be used.

📌 From official guidelines

Ensure spelling of applicant's name and parents' names matches between your birth proof and education certificates.

ℹ️ Requirements can change. Always check the official website before final submission.

📜From official guidelines

Birth certificate issued by the Registrar of Births & Deaths or Municipal Corporation is the preferred authoritative document (MEA Rules).
