Caste Certificate (Maharashtra) · What you need

Father's / Paternal Relative's Caste Certificate & LC

School Leaving Certificate, Caste Certificate, or Birth Register of father, brother, or paternal uncle.

Checklist Status

Open my checklist →

What is it?

Establishes that the paternal bloodline officially holds this caste in Maharashtra.

Why do I need it?

Crucial proof under Maharashtra lineage law.

ℹ️ Plain-language explanation from KAAMKAAGAZ, not an official legal statement.

✔️

What can you use?

This list is based on official rules. Requirements can vary depending on your situation.

  • Father's School Leaving Certificate showing caste entry
  • Real brother's or paternal uncle's Caste Certificate
  • Paternal grandfather's school / death record
📋

Format & preparation

📄 Submission type

Color scanned copy uploaded to portal.

✍️ Self-attestation

Self-attested by applicant/father.

💻 Digital / scan copy

PDF.

📐 File format

PDF under 2MB.

ℹ️ Requirements can change. Always check the official website before final submission.

📜From official guidelines

Maternal relatives (mama, nana, mother) are strictly inadmissible for caste claim in Maharashtra.
